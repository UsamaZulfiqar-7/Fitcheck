# Sample data (SYNTHETIC)

All files in this folder were generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py` with a fixed random seed.
Companies, job postings and numbers are **fictional** and exist only for development and demonstration.
They are not real vacancies and must not be presented as real market data.

- `synthetic_jobs.csv`: sample job postings (one row per job; skills separated by `|`).
- `skill_mappings.csv`: skill taxonomy with aliases and how often each skill appears in the synthetic jobs.

FitCheck does not need any real resume dataset: users upload their own resume.
